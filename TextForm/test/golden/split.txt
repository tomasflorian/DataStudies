split: the text cut into blocks at blank lines
dictionary: Block:cut "\n\n"
each input (as JSON), then the blocks it gives as [block], newlines in them as ⏎

"a\nb\n\nc"
  [a⏎b]
  [c]
"\n\na\n\n\n\nb\n\n"
  [a]
  [b]
"a\n\n\nb"
  [a]
  [⏎b]
"a\n\n  \n\nb"
  [a]
  [b]
"no separator"
  [no separator]
"\n\n"
  (none)
